1. **Sales Strategy & Process Design**

2. **Revenue Model Optimization**

## Revenue Model Framework

### Freemium Structure

#### **Free Tier**
- Basic CLI with 4 standard templates
- Limited AI suggestions (10 per month)
- Community support only
- Basic progressive disclosure

**Purpose**: User acquisition and product validation

#### **Pro Tier** ($29/month per developer)
- All 12 templates + AI-generated variants
- Unlimited AI features (Architecture Advisor, Smart Validation)
- Priority support and documentation
- Advanced progressive disclosure with learning

**Purpose**: Individual developer monetization

#### **Team Tier** ($99/month per 5 developers)
- Everything in Pro
- Team learning and shared preferences
- Custom template creation with AI assistance
- Team analytics and usage insights
- Slack/Teams integration

**Purpose**: Development team collaboration

#### **Enterprise Tier** (Custom pricing starting $500/month)
- Everything in Team
- AI Migration Assistant for legacy code
- Custom AI training on company patterns
- SSO and enterprise security
- Dedicated support and success management
- Custom blueprint marketplace

**Purpose**: Large organization transformation

Team Growth → Enterprise Needs → Sales Qualified Lead → Enterprise Deal

### Objection Handling

#### **"We already have templates/scaffolding"**
**Response**: "Static templates vs. intelligent AI that learns your patterns and provides architectural guidance. Show me your current approach and I'll demonstrate the productivity difference."

#### **"Our developers won't adopt new tools"**
**Response**: "Progressive disclosure starts simple and grows with expertise. 90% of users report easier onboarding than their current process. Can we run a small pilot to prove adoption?"

**Response**: "Our AI follows established Go patterns and includes human review workflows. All code is transparent and auditable. Would you like to see our quality assurance process?"

#### **"Budget/timing concerns"**
**Response**: "The cost of inconsistent architecture and slow development typically exceeds our pricing by 10x. What if we structured a pilot to prove ROI before full commitment?"
